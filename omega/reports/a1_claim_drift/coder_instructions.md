# Codebook (code each text on its own; texts are unrelated to each other as far as you know)

For each text, code 0/1 on items 1-9 and a category on item 10. Code only what THIS text states
about THIS event. A claim counts if the text asserts it happened (even if attributed to a witness).
A claim explicitly negated ("no traces were found", "no radiation") is 0. OCR noise is expected.

1 Ground mark or depression (imprints, marks, holes, flattened/swirled area, landing traces)
2 Burn, scorch or heating (burned grass/ground, charring, heat felt on objects/ground, melted)
3 Residue, deposit or material recovered (substance, metal fragment, oily/powdery deposit, sample taken of such)
4 Radiation reading (Geiger/counter reading, radioactivity measured, elevated or not — code 1 only if a reading was reported as elevated/abnormal OR the text says radiation was found; a reading reported normal is 0)
5 Vegetation effect (wilting, dehydration, broken branches, growth change, damaged plants/trees)
6 Animal effect (animals disturbed, injured, killed, panicked)
7 Human injury or physiological effect (burns, illness, paralysis, eye irritation, etc.)
8 Laboratory analysis stated (any sample sent to / analysed by a lab, university, expert)
9 Laboratory result stated as anomalous (analysis reported something unexplained/unusual)
10 Number of distinct quantitative values given about the event (measurements, sizes, durations, counts): "0", "1", "2-5", ">5"

Also give, for every item coded 1, a verbatim quote of at most 30 words from the text supporting it.
