# Non-human intelligence: what Project Omega's evidence supports

*Project Omega, session 5, 2026-11-26. Handoff §5 item 7, written last and from the
vault only. Every number below is quoted from a committed report and cited by file.
Nothing new is computed here. The handoff calls this the "NHI Proof Report". The
title is changed because the evidence doesn't support "proof", and keeping the old
title would misstate the result.*

---

## Verdict

**Nothing in this project's public/FOIA evidence requires non-human intelligence,
and nothing excludes it.** Across five sessions, every test built to detect an
NHI-specific signature either returned a null at stated power or traced back to
an artefact of the data or the method. Three things do survive:

1. **A small residue of genuinely unexplained cases in the one official dataset
   that separates "unexplained" from "under-documented".** GEIPAN class D is 106 of
   3,404 cases (3.1%). That residue **shrinks** as the identification toolkit
   improves, and it is the **least documented** part of the file.
2. **One physical-trace case with a real biochemical gradient** (Trans-en-Provence,
   1981). The gradient is real, but no test distinguishes the mechanism behind it.
3. **Specific, checkable gaps in the government record.** The AAWSAP capstone
   assessment, DOW-UAP-D66–D73, and one FY2010 report are missing. They are
   requestable by line item. A gap is not a finding about the phenomenon.

The analogy that fits is a court case where every witness turns out to be
repeating every other witness. That doesn't prove the event didn't happen. It does
mean the testimony can't carry the verdict, and the case rests on the two or
three exhibits that were collected independently.

## 2. What the government record shows

- **AAWSAP was a literature survey.** 1 of 37 DIRDs mentions UAP at all. The
  open-to-closed citation ratio is 27.5:1 (`dird_corpus.md`). The one exception,
  D128, concludes that injuries are attributable to **terrestrial** RF/microwave
  technology "advanced beyond public information".
- **All 26 FY2009 reports are released**, confirmed by the government's own
  author-pseudonym namespace (26/26, `aawsap_contract_audit.md`).
- **The capstone Integrated Threat Assessment is absent.** That's CLIN 0004
  ($240,000) and CLIN 1004 ($220,200). Its base-year due date (30 Sep 2009)
  passed before DIA's own deliverables review, which doesn't mention it. BAASS,
  on 23 Nov 2010, still described a threat assessment as future work (D175
  pp.98–99). That fits "never produced" at least as well as "withheld".
  **FOIA Letter 14, drafted, not filed.**
- **Redactions protect force composition and personnel, not observations.**
  Callsign is withheld at 0.88–0.91, personnel at 0.63, time at 0.09. The UAP
  observation line is released, including in the three gap-flanking reports
  OCR'd today (`redaction_slots.md`, `imgonly_ocr.md`).
- **Named-codename absences.** Zero document bodies contain *non-human biologic*,
  *crash retrieval*, *Immaculate Constellation*, *Kona Blue*, *moon dust*, *serpo*
  or *majestic* (`pursue_codenames_and_gaps.md`, deep-terms Track A–D). "Absent
  from the released corpus" is the claim. It isn't proof that nothing exists.

## 3. What would change the verdict

These are concrete and falsifiable, and each names the dataset that would decide it.

1. **A physical sample with chain of custody** from a D-class trace case, assayed
   against a matched control soil by an independent lab. Blocked today on
   custody, not chemistry (`phase9_soil_assay_targets.md`).
2. **The 56 undisclosed GEIPAN D dossiers** (CADA request drafted). If the
   reopened, unpublished D cases contain multi-sensor or instrumented data
   absent from published D cases, the residue becomes evidence, not just
   absence of information.
3. **The POSS-I Earth-shadow deficit tested against fallout fogging.** If plate
   transients avoid the shadow *and* show no correlation with atmospheric-test
   fallout timing, the prosaic alternative fails. This needs arxiv.org and the
   plate metadata, and arxiv.org is still blocked from this environment.
4. **Field-level coordinates for modern ground-contact reports.** With them,
   Track 2's self-controlled design has real power. Settlement centroids don't.
5. **The capstone assessment, or its acceptance and payment records.** If it was
   delivered and concludes something non-prosaic, that's the government's own
   threat judgement. If it was never delivered, the program's own deliverable
   chain ended without one.

## 4. Red team of this report

1. **Absence of evidence is weighted here by power, not assumed.** Where a test
   was underpowered (Track 2, GEIPAN C-vs-D witness tests), it is labelled
   underpowered, not null. A reader who says "underpowered nulls are being
   counted against NHI" has a point only where the label is missing. Check the
   table: it isn't.
2. **The corpus is what was released or published.** If decisive material exists
   and is classified, this project can't see it by design. The redaction analysis
   bounds what the *released* documents withhold. It says nothing about documents
   never released.
3. **Selection runs both ways.** GEIPAN's D-class is defined by informational
   adequacy, so the cases most likely to be anomalous are also the ones the
   criterion makes hardest to test from text. That's a structural ceiling, not a
   finding against the residue.
4. **Single-reader judgements.** The DIRD area assignment and the Track 2
   ground-contact selection were made by one reader. Both are published with
   their framing strings or reasons so a second reader can redo them.
5. **Prior-error rate is real.** This project has corrected its own numbers
   repeatedly (seed averaging, TEP telex values, D182/D110 identity, an offset
   bug in today's Track 2). Treat any single number as provisional until it has
   been reproduced from the vault by script.
